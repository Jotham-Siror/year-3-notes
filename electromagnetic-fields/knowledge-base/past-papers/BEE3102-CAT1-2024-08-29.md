### Q1b · 13 marks · → `01-wave-characteristics-1` §4, §5, §7; `_reference-old-cohort/05-poynting-vector`

> [q] A plane electromagnetic wave having frequency of $100MHz$ is travelling in unbounded lossless
> dielectric having $\mu_r = 1$ and $\varepsilon_r = 4$. The time averaged Poynting vector of the wave is
> $5\ W/m^2$. Find
> i. The phase velocity $v_p$
> ii. The phase shift constant $\beta$ of the wave
> iii. The intrinsic impedance of the medium
> iv. The $E_{rms}$ and $H_{rms}$ of the wave
>
> *(13 marks)*

```yaml
given:
  frequency: {value: 100, unit: MHz}
  medium: "unbounded lossless dielectric"
  relative_permeability: {symbol: "μ_r", value: 1}
  relative_permittivity: {symbol: "ε_r", value: 4}
  time_averaged_poynting_vector: {value: 5, unit: "W/m²"}
  chain: "the four parts are a ladder and must be done in order — v_p from ε_r, then β = ω/v_p, then
          η = η₀/√ε_r, and only then the rms fields out of the time-averaged power density and η."
  why_this_one_matters: "it is the only question in the folder that runs power density BACKWARDS to a field
                         strength. Every other paper gives E and asks for power."
  marks_note: "13 marks are allocated to (b) as a whole, not per sub-part. Assume roughly 3 each with the
               last carrying 4, but say that is an assumption if you set it as a mock."
```

### Q2b · 20 marks · → `_reference-old-cohort/07-reflection-transmission`

> [q] Consider perfect dielectric in region 1 and 2 for which $\eta_1$ and $\eta_2$ are positive real
> quantities and $\alpha_1 = \sigma_1 = 0$. The intrinsic impedances in medium 1 and 2 respectively are given
> $\eta_1 = 300\Omega$, $\eta_2 = 100\Omega$ and $E_{xi1} = 100V$. Determine each of the following:
> i. Reflection coefficient
> ii. Transmission coefficient
> iii. Reflected wave in region 1
> iv. Magnetic field in region 1
> v. Reflected magnetic field
> vi. Transmitted electric field in region 2
> vii. Corresponding transmitted magnetic field in region 2
> viii. The incident power $P_{iav}$
> ix. The reflected power in region 1
> x. The transmitted power I region 2
>
> *(20 marks)*
> ⚠ VERIFY *("$E_{xi1} = 100V$" — the volt is not a unit of field intensity; it should be V/m — erratum P15.
> "The transmitted power **I** region 2" — "I" for "in" — erratum P16)*
