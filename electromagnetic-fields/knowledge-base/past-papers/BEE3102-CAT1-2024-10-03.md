### Q1b · → ❌ **absent from both knowledge bases**

> [q] The impedance at any point a long a transmission line measured from the generator is given by
>
> $$Z_L = Z_0\,\frac{Z_L \cos(\beta z) + jZ_0 \sin \beta z}{Z_0 \cos(\beta z) + jZ_L \sin \beta z}$$
>
> Using this equation, show how a transmission line can be used to realize each of the following components
> i. A capacitor;
> ii. An inductor;
> iii. A quarter wave transformer;
>
> ⚠ VERIFY *(the left-hand side is printed $Z_L$, but $Z_L$ also appears twice on the right — the equation as
> printed is circular. It should read $Z_{in}$, which is how the same equation is printed on page 3 of the
> 25 Oct 2024 exam — erratum P18. Also "at any point **a long**" for "along")*

```yaml
given:
  equation_as_printed: "Z_L = Z_0 (Z_L cos(βz) + jZ_0 sin βz) / (Z_0 cos(βz) + jZ_L sin βz)"
  equation_corrected: "Z_in = Z_0 (Z_L cos(βz) + jZ_0 sin βz) / (Z_0 cos(βz) + jZ_L sin βz)"
  see: erratum P18
  asked_for: "for each component, choose the terminating Z_L and the length z that make Z_in take the
              required form — the whole question is three special cases of one equation"
  coverage: "❌ transmission-line theory appears in neither WC1 nor _reference-old-cohort/"
```
