# QUESTION ONE

## (a) — 2 marks

*You are given two antennas, A and B, with vertical and elliptical polarization
respectively. You are setting up a communication system. One antenna is to be used as
a transmitter and the other as a receiver. With reason, explain which one you would
use as a receiver and which one as a transmitter.*

**Solution.** Transmitter: B (elliptical). Receiver: A (vertical).

Polarization of transmitter and receiver must match for maximum power transfer. An
elliptically polarized wave has an $\vec{E}$ component in every direction, so the
vertical receiving antenna will always pick up a signal whatever its orientation.

## (b)(i) — 2 marks

*Explain why transmission loss in a copper cable increases with increase in frequency.*

**Solution.**

$$\delta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi f\mu\sigma}}$$

Higher frequency gives a smaller skin depth, so current is confined to a thin surface
layer (skin effect). The smaller conducting area raises the resistance, and the
$I^2R$ loss rises with it.

## (b)(ii) — 2 marks

*Explain why a pure conductor can be used as an electromagnetic shield.*

**Solution.** A conductor has very large $\sigma$, so its skin depth is almost zero.
The wave is attenuated to nothing within that depth, so no field passes through.

## (c) — 2 marks

*Distinguish between Poynting theorem and Poynting vector.*

**Solution.**

- **Poynting vector** — a quantity: $\vec{P} = \vec{E}\times\vec{H}$, in W/m²,
  giving the magnitude and direction of power flow per unit area at a point.
- **Poynting theorem** — an equation: energy conservation for fields. Power entering a
  volume = power dissipated inside + rate of increase of stored energy.

## (d) — 4 marks

*Human exposure to electromagnetic radiation in air is regarded as safe if the power
density is less than 10 mW/m². Determine the corresponding electric field intensity.*

**Solution.** Air, so $\eta = \eta_0 = 377\ \Omega$.

$$P_{av} = \frac{1}{2}\frac{E_0^2}{\eta_0} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad E_0 = \sqrt{2P_{av}\eta_0}$$

$$E_0 = \sqrt{2(0.01)(377)} = \sqrt{7.54} = \boxed{2.75\ \text{V/m (peak)}}$$

$$E_{rms} = \frac{E_0}{\sqrt{2}} = 1.94\ \text{V/m}$$

**Unit caution.** The internationally used public RF exposure limit is about
**10 W/m²**, whose field figure is 61 V/m rms — a number that appears directly in
exposure guidelines. 10 mW/m² is a thousand times stricter than any real limit, so the
printed "m" may be a slip. If 10 W/m² is intended: $E_0 = 86.8$ V/m peak,
$E_{rms} = 61.4$ V/m. Answer as printed and add one line naming the alternative.

## (e) — 3 marks

*State three types of electric polarization.*

**Solution.** Linear, circular, elliptical.

| Type | Condition | Locus of the E tip |
|---|---|---|
| Linear | components in phase (0°) | straight line |
| Circular | equal amplitudes, 90° phase difference | circle |
| Elliptical | unequal amplitudes and/or general phase | ellipse |

Elliptical is the general case; linear and circular are its special limits.

*(If dielectric rather than wave polarization is meant: electronic, ionic,
orientational. Q1a is about antennas, so wave polarization is almost certainly wanted.)*

# QUESTION TWO

## (a) — 12 marks

*The electric field intensity in a dielectric medium ($\mu = \mu_0$,
$\varepsilon = \varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r$) is given by*

$$\vec{E} = 150\cos\left(10^8 t + 8x\right)\vec{a}_z\ \text{V/m}$$

**Read off first.**

$$E_0 = 150\ \text{V/m}, \qquad \omega = 10^8\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad \beta = 8\ \text{rad/m}$$

$$\vec{a}_E = \vec{a}_z, \qquad \vec{a}_p = -\vec{a}_x \quad (\text{plus sign in the bracket} \Rightarrow -x)$$

### (i) Dielectric constant

$$v = \frac{\omega}{\beta} = \frac{10^8}{8} = 1.25\times10^7\ \text{m/s}$$

$$\sqrt{\varepsilon_r} = \frac{c}{v} = \frac{3\times10^8}{1.25\times10^7} = 24$$

$$\boxed{\varepsilon_r = 576}$$

Direct alternative: $\sqrt{\varepsilon_r} = \beta c/\omega = (8)(3\times10^8)/10^8 = 24$.

### (ii) Intrinsic impedance

$$\eta = \frac{\eta_0}{\sqrt{\varepsilon_r}} = \frac{377}{24} = \boxed{15.7\ \Omega}$$

### (iii) Velocity of propagation

$$v = \frac{\omega}{\beta} = \boxed{1.25\times10^7\ \text{m/s}}$$

### (iv) Magnetic field intensity

$$H_0 = \frac{E_0}{\eta} = \frac{150}{15.71} = 9.55\ \text{A/m}$$

$$\vec{a}_H = \vec{a}_p \times \vec{a}_E = (-\vec{a}_x)\times\vec{a}_z = -(\vec{a}_x\times\vec{a}_z) = -(-\vec{a}_y) = \vec{a}_y$$

$$\boxed{\vec{H} = 9.55\cos\left(10^8 t + 8x\right)\vec{a}_y\ \text{A/m}}$$

Check: $\vec{a}_E\times\vec{a}_H = \vec{a}_z\times\vec{a}_y = -\vec{a}_x = \vec{a}_p$. ✓

### (v) Poynting vector

$$\vec{P} = \vec{E}\times\vec{H} = (150)(9.55)\cos^2\left(10^8t+8x\right)\left(\vec{a}_z\times\vec{a}_y\right)$$

$$\boxed{\vec{P} = -1432\cos^2\left(10^8 t + 8x\right)\vec{a}_x\ \text{W/m}^2}$$

$$P_{av} = \frac{1}{2}\frac{E_0^2}{\eta} = \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{150^2}{15.71} = \boxed{716.2\ \text{W/m}^2 \text{ along } -\vec{a}_x}$$

Consistency check: the cycle-average of $\cos^2$ is ½, and $1432/2 = 716$. ✓

## (b) — 3 marks

*If the constitutive parameters of copper are $\mu = 4\pi\times10^{-7}$ H/m and
$\sigma = 5.8\times10^{-7}$ mho/m, determine the frequency for which the skin depth in
copper is 0.01 mm.*

**Solution.** Using $\sigma = 5.8\times10^{7}$ S/m (see misprint note).

$$\delta = 0.01\ \text{mm} = 1\times10^{-5}\ \text{m}$$

$$\delta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi f\mu\sigma}} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad f = \frac{1}{\pi\mu\sigma\delta^2}$$

$$\mu\sigma = \left(4\pi\times10^{-7}\right)\left(5.8\times10^{7}\right) = 72.88$$

$$f = \frac{1}{\pi(72.88)(10^{-10})} = \frac{1}{2.290\times10^{-8}} = \boxed{43.7\ \text{MHz}}$$

# Formulas this paper needs

$$\delta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi f\mu\sigma}} = \sqrt{\frac{2}{\omega\mu\sigma}}, \qquad f = \frac{1}{\pi\mu\sigma\delta^2}, \qquad \alpha = \beta = \sqrt{\pi f\mu\sigma}$$

$$v = \frac{\omega}{\beta} = \frac{c}{\sqrt{\mu_r\varepsilon_r}}, \qquad \eta = \eta_0\sqrt{\frac{\mu_r}{\varepsilon_r}}, \qquad \eta_0 = 120\pi = 377\ \Omega$$

$$H_0 = \frac{E_0}{\eta}, \qquad \vec{a}_E\times\vec{a}_H = \vec{a}_p, \qquad \vec{a}_H = \vec{a}_p\times\vec{a}_E$$

$$\vec{P} = \vec{E}\times\vec{H}, \qquad P_{av} = \frac{1}{2}\frac{E_0^2}{\eta}, \qquad E_0 = \sqrt{2P_{av}\eta}$$

Sign rule: $\cos(\omega t - \beta z)$ travels in $+z$; $\cos(\omega t + \beta z)$ travels in $-z$.

Cyclic rule: $\vec{a}_x\times\vec{a}_y = \vec{a}_z$, $\vec{a}_y\times\vec{a}_z = \vec{a}_x$,
$\vec{a}_z\times\vec{a}_x = \vec{a}_y$; reverse the order, flip the sign.
