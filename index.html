Analogue Electronics I BEE 3103

Three tiers of source: the course’s own lecture notes set the scope, seven lesson documents go deeper, and four slide decks are unverified background. The index says which one wins.

Tier 1 — the course notes

  • 00 — Index Three-tier map and which source is authoritative where. Start here.
  • 01 — Matter, atoms and semiconductors Band gaps, intrinsic silicon, P-type and N-type doping.
  • 02 — Resistors and DC networks Ohm’s law through Thevenin, Norton and mesh analysis. The most arithmetic-dense range in the course.
  • 03 — Capacitors, inductors, transformers Reactance, time constants, turns ratio and losses.
  • 04 — Diodes Bias, the V–I characteristic, load line and Q point, three diode models.
  • 05 — Rectifiers, filters, regulation Half-wave to bridge, derived through to ripple factor; zener regulation.
  • 06 — Bipolar junction transistors Nine characteristic families, six biasing methods, a worked amplifier design.
  • 07 — Field-effect transistors JFET, DE MOSFET, enhancement NMOS, six bias schemes.

Tier 2 — the lesson documents (deeper, and sole source for 15–17)

  • 11 — Diodes · 12 — Rectifiers PIV, TUF and form factor, which the course notes never give.
  • 13 — BJT · 14 — FETs Solved bias formulas, transconductance, the Miller effect.
  • 15 — Fabrication and ICs Sole source. Wafer processes, the seven-mask bipolar and five-mask NMOS flows.
  • 16 — h-parameters Sole source. The two-port model and the six derived quantities.
  • 17 — Feedback and frequency response Sole source. Four topologies, the decibel, bandwidth and the corner frequencies.

Reference

  • Verification log 388 flags — the largest in the repository — plus eleven recurring failure modes.
  • Formula sheet Corrected forms, a cross-tier concordance and a 25-item memorise list.
  • Nomenclature A 47-row clash table. Two of the clashes change the answer rather than merely confusing.
  • Reference decks Tier 3 — four slide decks, mapped but not verified.

Digital Electronics II BEE 3102

Six chapter decks, 348 slides, read as page images and verified equation by equation. Three of the decks split across more than one file — the index maps them.

Knowledge base — all 10 topics

  • 00 — Index Coverage map, chapter-to-file map and dependency order. Start here.
  • 01 — Introduction and recap Integration scales, then Digital Electronics I recapped — flip-flops, registers, counters.
  • 02 — Digital logic families Logic levels, noise margins, fan-out, power — then DL, RTL, DTL, TTL and CMOS.
  • 03 — Semiconductor memory Arrays, SRAM, DRAM, decoding, and Hamming error correction.
  • 04 — Programmable logic devices PROM, PLA, PAL, CPLD and FPGA — and which array is programmable in each.
  • 05 — Analogue-to-digital conversion Sampling, quantisation, and the flash, SAR, dual-slope and sigma-delta converters.
  • 06 — Digital-to-analogue conversion Resolution, full-scale error, binary-weighted and R/2R ladder converters.
  • 07 — FSM fundamentals and analysis Mealy and Moore, state tables and diagrams, and the flip-flop excitation tables.
  • 08 — Sequential circuit design Sequence detectors, worked both ways, six machines start to finish.
  • 09 — State reduction and assignment Implication tables pass by pass, then assignment and four worked cases.
  • 10 — Algorithmic state machines ASM charts, SM blocks, PLA realisation, and two datapath controllers.

Reference

  • Verification log 81 flags. Read the “before a CAT” shortlist — the R/2R ladder formula is wrong by a factor of two.
  • Formula sheet Every equation in corrected form, with the printed version alongside.
  • Nomenclature 33 symbol clashes. Two of them change the answer rather than merely confusing.
  • Past papers CAT 1 2024, transcribed and worked. Half its marks came from the DAC half of Chapter 4 alone.

Engineering Mathematics III EMT 3101

Six topic documents, 66 pages — one typeset, five handwritten scans read as page images. Every stated answer recomputed; every ambiguous glyph re-read at 600 dpi.

Knowledge base — all 6 topics

  • 00 — Index Coverage map, dependency order, and the two page-numbering conventions. Start here.
  • 01 — Gamma and Beta functions The factorial extended; four Gamma properties; the Beta function in four equivalent forms.
  • 02 — CRV and the Beta distribution pdf, cdf, expectation and variance — then the Beta distribution built from them.
  • 03 — The Binomial theorem General expansion, the rth term, negative and fractional indices, percentage-change problems.
  • 04 — Maclaurin’s theorem Series from derivatives at the origin, numerical integration, and indeterminate limits.
  • 05 — Leibniz theorem nth derivatives of seven standard functions, then the same for a product.
  • 06 — Bessel’s equation The Frobenius solution in Gamma form, J₀ and J₁, and six recurrence formulas proved.

Reference

  • Verification log 24 flags. Five of the nine serious ones are a correct derivation followed by a wrong number copied from it.
  • Formula sheet Every equation in corrected form, tagged to its source page.
  • Nomenclature The clash table first — n alone carries five different meanings across the six topics.
  • Assessed work Assignment 1, transcribed and solved. Four of its five questions come from topic 01 alone.
