## What a knowledge base for this unit would have to contain

This is the main value of the folder. The one paper gives a usable inventory of the syllabus:

- **Sampling and sampling distributions** — the distribution of the sample mean, its mean and standard
  error, the effect of sample size, the shape of the sampling distribution when the population is skewed.
- **Sampling methods** — quota sampling in particular, described rather than calculated.
- **The normal distribution** — recovering μ and σ from two tail probabilities; standardising; tail areas.
- **Hypothesis testing** — one-sample tests on a mean, significance levels, what a rejection implies about
  the sample mean; two-sample comparison of means from paired experimental data.
- **Estimation** — point and interval estimates.
- **Correlation and regression** — from a nine-observation experimental data set.

Roughly: **inferential statistics for engineers**. Nothing in the EMT 3101 knowledge base overlaps it.

**⚠ No statistical tables were photographed with the paper**, and several questions need them (normal,
*t*). They were almost certainly a separate booklet — worth asking him for. Logged as **P2**.
