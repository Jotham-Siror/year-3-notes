# Engineering Mathematics IV — EMT 3201

**There is no knowledge base for this unit yet.** This folder exists because a past paper for it turned up
in the same PDF as the Year 3 Semester 1 papers, and it needed somewhere honest to live.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Unit** | EMT 3201 — Engineering Mathematics IV |
| **Sits** | Year 3, Semester 2 (the EMT 3101 papers are Semester 1) |
| **Knowledge base** | ❌ none — no lecture notes have been supplied |
| **What is here** | one end-of-semester paper, transcribed — see `knowledge-base/past-papers/` |

**Do not confuse this with EMT 3101.** They are different units with different content. EMT 3101 is Gamma
and Beta functions, Maclaurin, Leibniz, Bessel. EMT 3201, on the evidence of its one paper, is
**statistics** — sampling distributions, hypothesis testing, estimation, the normal distribution.

**If you are asked to teach or revise this unit:** say plainly that there is no knowledge base, and offer
either to build one from lecture notes he supplies, or to work from the past paper alone. Do not answer
from general knowledge as though it were his course material — the same rule that applies everywhere else
in this repository.

To build the KB, put lecture notes in `sources/` with a `SOURCES.md` manifest and follow
`../docs/kb-format.md`.
