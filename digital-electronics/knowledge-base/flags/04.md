# Verification flags — file 04, Programmable Logic Devices (CH3 slides 34–66)

Checked: every Boolean expression, K-map, programming table and fuse map in the range was
rederived independently in Python and compared cell by cell against the slide. That covers the
PROM truth table and fuse map (slides 42–43, 8 words × 8 bits), the PLA example and its
programming table (slide 45), both PLA worked examples (slides 46–47 and 48–49), and the PAL
example with its 12-row programming table and 10-column fuse map (slides 51–52). **All of the
arithmetic and all of the logic in this range is correct.** The seven entries below are labelling,
notation and typographical defects.

---

### V04-1 · CH3 slide 52

**Prints:** the PAL fuse map labels its ten AND-array columns twice. The row above the array reads
$A\;A'\;B\;B'\;C\;C'\;D\;D'\;w\;w'$; the row below the array reads
$A\;A'\;\mathbf{B'}\;B'\;C\;C'\;D\;D'\;w\;w'$.
**Should be:** the third column is $B$, as the top row has it. The bottom row prints $B'$ twice.
**Why:** product term 5 must realise $x$'s second term $BCD$, and its cross sits in column 3. Read
from the bottom labels it would be $B'CD$, which is a different function —
$B'CD = \sum(3,11)$ against $BCD = \sum(7,15)$, and only the latter completes
$x = \sum(7,8,\ldots,15)$. Product term 7 ($A'B$) has a cross in the same column and fails the same
way.
**Class:** substantive — a reader tracing the map from the bottom labels gets the wrong literal on
two of the twelve product terms. The top label row and the crosses together make the intended
reading unambiguous.

---

### C04-1 · CH3 slide 49

**Prints:** the fuse legend reads "$\times$ Fuse intact" / "**1** Fuse blown".
**Should be:** "$\times$ Fuse intact" / "$+$ Fuse blown", as the identical legend on slide 45
prints it.
**Why:** the bullet glyph has been substituted by the digit 1. It is actively misleading, because
slide 43 defines "$1 \rightarrow$ connection" — the opposite sense.
**Class:** cosmetic — the crosses in the figure still carry their normal meaning, and slide 45
gives the correct legend two examples earlier.

---

### C04-2 · CH3 slide 58

**Prints:** "All the flip-flops are connected to the common CLK input and all three-state buffers
are controlled by the **EO** input."
**Should be:** the **OE** (output enable) input.
**Why:** the macrocell figure one slide earlier labels the signal OE, and OE is the standard name
for an output enable. The two letters are transposed.
**Class:** cosmetic.

---

### C04-3 · CH3 slide 49

**Prints:** "We **inclement** F1 Using the PLA then invert it , as this is more economical".
**Should be:** "We **implement** $\bar{F_1}$ using the PLA then invert it, as this is more
economical."
**Why:** typing slip; the sentence is otherwise the point of the whole example.
**Class:** cosmetic.

---

### C04-4 · CH3 slide 66

**Prints:** homework problem 4(a) — "a binary multiplier that multiplies two 4-bit,"
**Should be:** "a binary multiplier that multiplies two 4-bit **numbers**".
**Why:** the noun is missing, so the clause has no object. The intended reading is unambiguous:
two 4-bit operands, hence an 8-input, 8-output truth table.
**Class:** cosmetic — but worth knowing before the problem is attempted, since the question asks for
a ROM size and the missing word is the thing being sized.

---

### C04-5 · CH3 slide 40

**Prints:** the PLD-notation figure is lettered (a), (b), (c), (d), (g), (h).
**Should be:** either (a)–(f), or the six panels relettered (a)–(f).
**Why:** parts (e) and (f) of the source figure have been dropped, but the lettering of the
remaining panels was not adjusted, so the sequence jumps.
**Class:** cosmetic — nothing in the notation is missing; the six panels shown cover cross, no
cross and junction dot for both AND and OR.

---

### C04-6 · CH3 slides 62 and 63

**Prints:** slide 62 (untitled) and slide 63 ("Cont…") carry the **same** figure — basic CLBs within
the global row/column programmable interconnects — and the same sentence of body text.
**Should be:** one slide.
**Why:** duplicated slide. Slide 62 has no title bar, which suggests 63 is the intended version.
**Class:** cosmetic — no content is lost or contradicted.

## Unreadable

None. Every slide in the range 34–66 was legible at 200 dpi. Two figures needed magnification before
they could be read reliably and are recorded here so the work is not repeated:

- **Slide 48**, the two lines of algebra under the K-maps: the overline glyphs are drawn with a
  drifting horizontal offset, so at page scale the bars appear to sit over the wrong letters. At
  7× magnification the terms resolve as
  $F_1 = \bar{A}\bar{B}C + \bar{A}B\bar{C} + A\bar{B}\bar{C}$ and
  $\bar{F_1} = AB + AC + BC + \bar{A}\bar{B}\bar{C}$, both of which agree with the printed maps.
  Nothing is wrong with the slide; it is a rendering artefact of the font.
- **Slide 52**, the bottom column-label row — see V04-1.
