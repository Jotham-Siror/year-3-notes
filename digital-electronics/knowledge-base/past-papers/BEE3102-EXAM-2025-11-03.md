## What this paper adds

### 1 · Two of its seven figures are the deck's own worked examples, redrawn

This is the finding to act on.

- **Figure 6 (Q4(a)) is the implication-table machine of ·CH5 slides 83–90**, which
  `09-state-reduction-and-assignment.md` §9.5 works over eight pages. The exam prints it as a state
  diagram instead of a table, and relabels $a \ldots h$ as $A \ldots H$; **all sixteen transitions
  and all eight outputs match, entry for entry** (checked one by one against the §9.5.1 table). Then
  Q4(a)(ii) asks the candidate to reduce it *by implication tables* — the exact method the slides
  work on that exact machine. **6 marks that are already fully solved in the knowledge base.**
- **Figure 7 (Q4(b)) is the 0101 / 1001 recogniser of ·CH5 slides 75–81**, which
  `09-state-reduction-and-assignment.md` §9.3 works as "Reduction by inspection". Fifteen states
  $A \ldots P$ with the letter O skipped, reset after every four inputs, $Z = 1$ on 0101 or 1001 —
  identical, including the leaf outputs at J and L. **And the exam figure is correct where the slide
  is not:** flag **V09-1** records that ·CH5 slides 77 and 78 label the node reached from $B$ on
  $X = 1$ as a *second $D$*, so the printed deck shows two nodes called $D$ and none called $E$.
  Figure 7 of this paper labels it **E**. If the examiner drew the figure from a corrected master,
  that is worth knowing.

Together those two questions are **15 marks — the whole of Question Four — sitting on material the
knowledge base has already worked and verified.** Read §9.3 and §9.5 before anything else on this
paper.

assume the CAT 2 form of the question.

Every mark on this paper lands on taught, verified material. The 2024 exam put 9 marks outside the
decks (the partitioning method, 4; a Verilog full adder, 5). **Neither appears here.** Q4(a)(ii) asks
for implication tables and does *not* offer partitioning as an alternative, and there is no HDL
question at all. One paper is not a trend, but it is worth knowing that the two untaught topics of
2024 did not return in 2025.

### 5 · Defects to know before working it

- **Figure 3 is wrong in exactly one place** and it is the same place as the 2024 exam's Figure 2 —
  the `1/1` self-loop on state 01. As printed the machine also fires on `11`, so Q1(g)'s "the
  sequence detected" has no single answer (erratum P19, cross-referring to **P8**).
- **Table 1 names a state S0 that the table does not contain** (erratum P20, cross-referring to
  **P5**).
- **Figure 4 gives no bit labels at all** — four bare input terminals with 120 k, 60 k, 30 k and
  15 k against them, and nothing to say which one the leading 1 of "1101" belongs to (erratum
  **P18** ★, the worst defect on this paper).
