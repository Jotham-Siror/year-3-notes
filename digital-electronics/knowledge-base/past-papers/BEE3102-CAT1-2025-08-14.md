verbatim into a second cohort is not a coincidence; it means the question bank is being reused
without re-typesetting.

**Two CAT 1 papers a year apart agree that roughly half a CAT 1 is the DAC half of Chapter 4** — that
is twenty slides carrying half a paper, twice. Chapters 5 and 6 score zero on both, exactly as on the

### 3 · The one question with no antecedent is the ADC one

Q1 — *sampling, quantization, encoding, sampling rate* — is 2 marks and is the first appearance of
`05-analogue-to-digital-conversion.md` anywhere in this folder. All four terms are taught: §5.2 names
the three stages of the conversion process, §5.3 defines the sampling rate and the Nyquist criterion,
§5.6 is quantisation and encoding. **Note the spelling.** The knowledge base writes *quantisation*
throughout (the decks' own spelling); the paper writes *Quantization*. Searching the KB for the
paper's spelling finds nothing — that is a spelling difference, not a coverage gap.

### 4 · No coverage gap, with one qualification

Every mark maps onto taught, verified material. The only doubt is Q2's second circuit, which is not
one of the four bipolar families the decks draw (diode logic §11, RTL §12, DTL §13, TTL §14); naming
it may fall on the **ECL / plain PMOS-NMOS** row of the gap map in `00-index.md`. That is at most 2
of the 4 marks on Q2. **No identification is made in this file** — the figure carries a defect that
makes one transistor inert (erratum P17 → see P13 on the 2024 exam).

### 5 · One question sits directly on the deck's worst defect

Q8's ladder is the R/2R network of `06-digital-to-analogue-conversion.md` §6.6, and the index leads
its "read before a CAT" list with **V06-9**: ·CH4 slides 44 and 47 print the bit weight
$V_S/2^{\,n-i}$ where the deck's own $D_0 \ldots D_{n-1}$ numbering requires

$$V_{\text{out}}(D_i) = -\frac{V_S}{2^{\,n-1-i}}$$

**Every R/2R value the deck gives is a factor of two low.** Q8 gives $V_R = 5$ V and asks for a
specific switch pattern; a candidate who revised from slide 44 as printed will be out by exactly a
factor of two. Read `../_verification-log.md` § 2 before working this question, and note that the
paper's node labelling ($A_0 \ldots A_3$ with $S_0$ the LSB) matches the deck's $D_0 \ldots D_{n-1}$
convention, so V06-9 applies directly.

⚠ VERIFY *(one "percentage resolution" is asked for in (a), but (b) treats the converter as BCD-input
