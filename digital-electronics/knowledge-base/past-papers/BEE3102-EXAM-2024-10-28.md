full. Work the CAT 1 answers until they are automatic.

### 3 · Question 5(a) is the deck's own homework, examined

reference voltage $V_{REF}$ is 8V. (i) Determine the number of comparators required for this Flash
ADC. (ii) Calculate the voltage resolution of the ADC. (iii) Determine the digital output for an

### 4 · Where the marks fall outside the knowledge base — 9 marks, quantified

- **Q4(a)(i) — the partitioning method, 4 marks.** ·CH5 slide 82 names three methods for finding
  equivalent states: inspection, **partitioning**, and implication tables. The decks work
  *inspection* (§9.3) and *implication tables* (§9.5) at length. **Partitioning is named and never
  worked** — anywhere, in any deck. The only partition refinement in the knowledge base is our own
  `[added]` verification tool, run in software to check the decks' answers; it is not a procedure a
  student could write out under examination conditions. **This is a new entry for the gap map in
  `00-index.md` § Coverage and gaps**, and it is worth exactly as many marks as the implication-table
  part sitting beside it.
- **Q5(b) — a Verilog full adder, 5 marks.** Verilog appears in this knowledge base twice, both in
  `01-introduction-and-recap.md`: a screenshot in the ·CH1 slide 8 montage, and ·CH1 slide 32's
  one-line description ("netlist description with built-in primitive gates, internal nets declared as
  wires, used for simulation, timing analysis, test analysis and logic synthesis"). **No syntax, no
  module, no worked example exists in any of the 348 slides.** Five marks of HDL coding on a unit
  that never teaches HDL coding.

Both gap questions sit in **optional** questions (Q4 and Q5), so a candidate could avoid all 9 marks
by answering Q2 and Q3. Worth saying out loud when planning a mock.

### 5 · Question 1(d) is Question 3(a) with the answer already drawn

Figure 2 on page 2 is a four-state Mealy machine. Every transition in it matches the standard
**overlapping 1001 detector** exactly — which is what Q3(a) asks the candidate to design from
scratch, for 15 marks. **Except one arc.** The self-loop on state 01 is printed `1/1` where the
detector requires `1/0`; as printed the machine also asserts Z on the sequence **11**, so Q1(d) —
*"What is the sequence detected…?"* — has no single correct answer. Verified by simulation in Python
(2026): exactly one of the eight edges differs from the canonical machine, and the printed machine
fires on `11`, `1001` and six other suffixes. See erratum **P8** — the worst defect on this paper.
