## What this paper adds

**It is the only evidence in this knowledge base of what the course actually examines, and it
contradicts the index's triage table on two counts.**

1. **Fabrication is worth a quarter of the CAT.** Items (f) and (g) — ion implantation, and the step
   sequence for an enhancement-only NMOS — are **10 of 40 marks**. The index's topic map records
   fabrication as "❌ two passing lines" in tier 1, closed only by `15`, and files `15` under
   "Descriptive, so learn the lists." That is right about the *kind* of learning and badly wrong about
   the *weight*: a quarter of this CAT rests on a document the course's own lecture notes do not teach.
   `15` also carries its own documented hole (Articles 67.3–67.12, the scales of integration, never
   delivered), so a question that strayed into SSI/LSI/VLSI would have no source at all.
2. **The common-collector characteristics are examinable.** Item (h) is 5 marks on the CC static and
   transfer families — circuit, curves and equations. The index lists ·J p68–p70 under **"Lowest
   value"**. Correct that entry when the past-paper register is written.

**Where the paper lands on a defect.** Item (e) is 5 marks on the enhancement-only NMOS, and ·J p91 —
the primary notes' page on exactly that device — is ranked **first** in the verification log's worst
offenders: it illustrates the E-only MOSFET with the DE MOSFET's static characteristics and narrates it
with the JFET's operating story. Teach item (e) from **`14` (·L4 p20–p21)**, which the log records as
settling it, and say out loud that ·J p91 is wrong. Item (d), the FET tree, must not be drawn from ·J
p91's bullets either.

**What it does not examine.** Nothing at all from `02` (dc network theorems), `03` (passives),
`05` (rectifier derivations), `16` ($h$-parameters) or `17` (feedback and frequency response). Four of
those five are the index's "highest value" rows. A CAT is a mid-semester instrument and this one stops
at the FET — but note that the October examination six weeks later put **59 of its 90 printed marks**
on `15`, `16`, `17` and `14` §4.8, i.e. precisely the material this CAT ignores.

**Recurrence.** Five of these eight items reappear on `BEE3106-EXAM-2024-10-22.md` — (b) as Q2(c),
(c) as Q1(d), (e) as Q3(c), (f) as Q4(a), (g) as Q5(b) — carrying **25 of this paper's 40 marks**. All
eight reappear verbatim on `BEE3106-CAT-2025-09-09.md`. Two papers is a small sample; a CAT reissued
unchanged a year later, five of whose items were lifted into the same year's final examination, is not.
