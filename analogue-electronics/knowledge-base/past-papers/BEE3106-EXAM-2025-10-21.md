## What this paper adds

**It is the second half of the evidence that this unit's examination lives in tier 2** — and it is
gentler about it than the 2024 paper, which matters.

| Sole-source material | Questions | Marks | Share of 90 | 2024 exam |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `16` $h$-parameters | 1e, 2a, 3a | **13** | 14 % | 11 |
| `17` feedback, multistage, frequency response | 1f[→g], 2b, 3c, 4d | **12** | 13 % | 9 |

| **Total on material tier 1 does not teach** | | **43** | **48 %** | **59 (66 %)** |

The share fell from two thirds to a half, and the internal balance moved: **fabrication halved (21 → 11)
while $h$-parameters and feedback both rose (11 → 13, 9 → 12)**. Across the two examinations the three
sole-source files carry **21 + 11 = 32**, **11 + 13 = 24** and **9 + 12 = 21** marks respectively. On
two papers, `15`, `16` and `17` between them are worth 77 of 180 printed marks. Whatever the index's
tier table says about precedence, **a student who has not read `15`, `16` and `17` cannot pass this
unit.**

**Two questions land squarely on damaged or unsettled KB material — quantify it for him.**

- **Q4(a), 6 marks**, asks for the three regions of the JFET drain characteristic at $V_{GS}=0$ and the
  behaviour of $I_D$ in each. The primary notes' JFET characteristic pages are **·J p87, p88 and p89 —
  all three carry opaque redaction blocks**, and the Gap map records that contrast recovery found
  nothing underneath. `14` must supply this. Six marks on three damaged pages.
- **Q4(c), 4 marks**, is a JFET self-bias Q-point with $V_P = -5$ V supplied *explicitly and with its
  sign*. That is a mercy: `JV7.1` / `V4.2` — worst offender #5 — is the $V_P$ sign collision that
  **neither tier can settle**, because both sources use both signs. The paper handing him the sign is
  the single most useful thing on it. Teach the check anyway: substituting the wrong sign into
  Shockley's equation returns $I_D > I_{DSS}$, which is impossible.
- **Q2(c)(vii)** asks for $K_\beta$. `13` is the file that carries $K_\beta$ and the β-rule, and the
  Gap map records **printed pp. 2205–2222, §57.24 — the β-rule, cited twice and never stated — as an
  open ❌**. Check before teaching this part whether `13`'s $K_\beta$ definition survives in the file or
  whether it is one of the two dangling citations. If it is dangling, this is the one part of this
  paper the knowledge base may genuinely not answer.

**What it examines that 2024 did not.** Oxidation and the SiO₂ film (3), etching (1), voltage-divider
bias as a *design* (6), the stability factor and $K_\beta$ (part of 8), the common-collector
$h$-parameter set worked **both exactly and approximately** (5), zener versus avalanche breakdown (2),
the JFET drain characteristic's three regions (6), a **biased** parallel clipper with two diodes and two
bias cells (3), and gain sensitivity as a percentage (2). The 2024 paper had none of these.

**What both examinations share.** Czochralski (4 marks on each, verbatim), ion implantation (5 → 3,
reworded), the transformer-coupled two-stage amplifier of Fig. 5(a)/Fig. 2(b) — *the same artwork*, with
$\beta$ moved from 50 to 75 and the allocation from 3 to 4 — the $h$-parameter exact-analysis question
(CE and CB in 2024, CC in 2025), a rectifier numerical (7 → 10 marks), a clipper (3 → 3), a zener
question (5 → 4 across two parts) and negative feedback (3 → 12). **Nine of the fifteen question areas
recur.** Two papers is a small sample, but the recurrence is not marginal: on topic, roughly 55 of the
90 marks in 2025 fall in areas the 2024 paper also examined.

**And the CAT link.** Item 1b[→f] — "Using a graphical plot for voltage vs current, describe the concept
of forward biasing and reverse biasing a P-N junction diode" (4 marks) — is the 2024/2025 CAT's item (a)
with "circuit diagrams and" deleted. Six of the CAT's eight items have now been examined again in a
final.

**Not examined on either paper:** anything at all from `02` — Ohm's law, the colour code, series/parallel
reduction, Thevenin, Norton, mesh analysis. That is a quarter of the lecture notes and the index's
top-ranked triage row, and it has produced **zero marks across two examinations and two CATs**. Say so
plainly before he spends revision time there.
