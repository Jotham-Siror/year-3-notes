## What this paper adds

**It is the proof of the index's central claim, and it is more extreme than the index expects.**

The index says "Tier 1 stops at the FET" — no $h$-parameter analysis, no feedback theory, no frequency
response, no fabrication, no $g_m$. This examination puts **59 of its 90 printed marks on exactly that
material**:

| Sole-source material | Questions | Marks | Share of 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `15` fabrication and ICs | 1f, 4a, 4c, 5b | **21** | 23 % |
| `16` $h$-parameters | 1h, 4b | **11** | 12 % |
| `17` feedback, multistage, frequency response | 1g, 5a, 5c | **9** | 10 % |
| `11`-only — barrier voltage from doping, zener regulator design | 1b, 2a | **8** | 9 % |
| `12`-only — voltage doubler, PIV, clippers | 1c, 2b(iv), 2c | **7** | 8 % |
| `14` §4.8 — $g_m$, $r_d$, the CD/CS gains | 1e | **3** | 3 % |
| **Total on material tier 1 does not teach** | | **59** | **66 %** |

A candidate who revised only from the 100-page lecture notes could not attempt two thirds of this
paper. That is the single most useful sentence in this folder. **`15`, `16` and `17` are not
"supporting" reading for this unit; they are two thirds of the final examination.**

**Nothing on this paper is absent from the knowledge base.** Every one of the 21 parts maps to a topic
file. The exposure is not a hole — it is a *precedence* problem: the index's tier table tells the reader
that "scope comes from tier 1", and on this evidence that is wrong for the examination even though it
may be right for the CAT.

**Three questions land on logged defects.** Say so out loud when teaching them:

- **Q5(c)** — the lower cut-off frequency at $C_1$, at $C_2$ and *for the amplifier* — is exactly the
  question `V7.17` destroys. ·L7 p24 takes the amplifier's lower cut-off as the **minimum** of the
  individual corner frequencies; it is the **maximum**. A student revising from that page answers Q5(c)
  backwards for the third mark.
- **Q3(c)** is the enhancement-only NMOS again, and ·J p91 (`JV7.2`/`JV7.3`) is a page about the wrong
  device. Teach from `14`.
- **Q2(b)(iv)** asks for the PIV of a half-wave rectifier. ·L2 p7 (`V2.8`) prints PIV $= 2V_{sm}$ for
  the half-wave case — the centre-tap answer — and tier 1 never computes PIV at all, so there is no
  second opinion inside the primary notes.

**Two questions sit on damaged pages.** Q3(b)'s two-supply emitter bias is `13` §3.15's territory, where
`V3.12` prints an $I_E$ formula that contains no base resistor; ·J p71 has the correct form. Q5(a) needs
the decibel, whose definition in `17` is `[added]` because printed pp. 2302–2306 are missing from the
source document.

**What it does not examine.** Not one mark from `02` (Ohm's law, colour code, Thevenin, Norton, mesh) —
the index's "most arithmetic-dense range in the course" and its top-ranked triage row. `03` appears only
implicitly, in the turns ratios of Fig. 2(b) and Fig. 5(a). If the triage table is rewritten from
past-paper evidence, `02` drops and `15` rises.

**Recurrence with the CAT six weeks earlier.** Five items are lifted from `BEE3106-CAT-2024-09-17.md`:

figures; only the mark allocations move (5 → 4, 5 → 3, 5 → 4, 5 → 5, 5 → 9). **25 of the CAT's 40 marks
