### What to do with that — and what not to do with it

**State it as what it is: two papers.** Two end-of-semester papers, thirteen months apart, sharing 70 of
90 marks with no rewording. That is a striking amount of reuse and it is the strongest revision signal in
this repository — **but it is a sample of two, and one of the two is the only other final we hold.** It
does not license skipping anything. Three specific cautions:

1. **`05` Leibniz went to zero.** It was 4 marks in 2024 (Q1(e)) and appears nowhere in 2025. A file that
   drops off one paper is not a file that has left the syllabus — Leibniz is still on the printed formula
   sheet of both papers.
2. **The reuse is not uniform.** Question Two and Question Five were reprinted *identically, in the same
   slots, for the same marks*. Question One was rebuilt: three of its six parts are new. If a pattern is
   forming, it is that **Q2 and Q5 are stable and Q1 is where the new material goes.**
3. **The four new questions are not new topics** — Bessel, CRV, Legendre and Beta were all already
   examinable. What is new is the *task*: Legendre by decomposition rather than by Rodrigues, Beta by a
   reduction formula rather than by direct read-off.

### Q3b · 5 marks · → `01-gamma-and-beta-functions` §5.4–5.5

> [q] Show clearly that *(5 Marks)*
>
> $$\int_0^{1}x^{m-1}(1-x)^{p-1}\,dx = \frac{1}{n}B\left(\frac{m}{n},\,p\right), \qquad n \ne 0.$$
>

>
> $$\int_0^{1}x^{5}\left(1-x^{3}\right)^{2}dx$$

"hence" cannot be done — the bracket should be $(1-x^{n})^{p-1}$ — erratum **P13** ★)*

```yaml

  identity_as_printed: "∫₀¹ x^(m-1) (1-x)^(p-1) dx = (1/n) B(m/n, p),  n ≠ 0"
  application: "∫₀¹ x⁵ (1-x³)² dx"
  asked_for: ["show the identity", "hence the exact value of the integral"]
legibility: partly obscured        # see the artefact note at the head of this file
  detail: "the printed exponent on (1-x) and the second argument of B are both overwritten in blue
           ballpoint. Blue-channel isolation at 7× reads the PRINTED glyph as p in both places, and the
           blue as an inserted n. Recorded as a partly obscured reading, not a clean one."
data_checked_here:                 # MINE — sympy, 2026-09-03
  correct_identity: "∫₀¹ x^(m-1) (1 - x^n)^(p-1) dx = (1/n) B(m/n, p)"
```
