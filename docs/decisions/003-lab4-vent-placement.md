# 003 — Lab 4: fan low on the front wall, vent high on the back wall
Date: 2026-10-12 · Supersedes: the first build's openings centred on the Z-faces
"Bottom Z-face" and "top Z-face" are read as low on the front (Z = 0) wall and high on the back (Z = 0.25 m) wall, both 0.20 × 0.20 m and centred across the width, because Y is the height in Step 1. Vents in the floor or roof would have no path past the baseplate, which fills the whole X-Z section. With the openings centred, the jet passed 65 mm above the modules (196 °C at 0.02 m³/s) and Step 7 had no meaningful answer.
