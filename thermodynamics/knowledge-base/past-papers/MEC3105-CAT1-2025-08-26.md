## Question Four · 8 marks · → `02-first-law` §2.9, §2.11 · ✅ **the only question inside the KB**

> [q] Air at 1.02 *bar*, 22°C, initially occupying a cylindrical volume of 0.015 $m^3$, is compressed
> reversibly and adiabatically by a piston to a pressure of 6.8 *bar*, Calculate
> **a)** The final temperature, *(2 marks)*
> **b)** The final volume, and *(2 marks)*
> **c)** The total work done on the mass of air in the cylinder. *(4 marks)*
> ⚠ VERIFY *(part (c) needs the specific gas constant to get the mass, and the front page prints it as
> "287 J/mol.K" — the wrong basis by a factor of ~29 — erratum **P16**)*

*⚠ **The V8 trap is in part (c).** `02-first-law` §2.3 records that FL states two contradictory sign
conventions for $W$ (s13 versus s30) and that its own worked example mixes them and lands a factor of five
out (**V8**). This question asks specifically for the work done **on** the air — the sign FL cannot keep
straight. Work it as $W_{\text{on}} = m\,c_v\,(T_2 - T_1)$, which is positive for a compression, and say
out loud which convention is in use.*

```yaml

  substance: air
  initial: {pressure: {value: 1.02, unit: bar}, temperature: {value: 22, unit: "°C"}, volume: {value: 0.015, unit: "m³"}}
  process: "reversible adiabatic (isentropic) compression by a piston — closed system"
  final_pressure: {value: 6.8, unit: bar}
  asked_for: ["final temperature", "final volume", "total work done ON the air"]
  constants_supplied_on_the_front_page:
    gamma: 1.4
    c_v: {printed: "0.718 J/kg.K", correct: "0.718 kJ/kg·K", see: erratum P16}
    R: {printed: "287 J/mol.K", correct: "287 J/kg·K", see: erratum P16}
  route: >
    T₂ = T₁(P₂/P₁)^((γ−1)/γ) ; V₂ = V₁(P₁/P₂)^(1/γ) ; m = P₁V₁/(R T₁) ; W_on = m c_v (T₂ − T₁),
    or equivalently W_on = (P₂V₂ − P₁V₁)/(γ − 1).
  well_posed: >
    ✅ YES — three independent data fix the initial state, one more fixes the final, and γ, c_v and R are
    all printed. It is the only fully self-contained question on the paper. Only the UNITS of two of the
    three constants are wrong, and both are recoverable by inspection.

  final_volume: {value: 0.003869, unit: "m³"}
  mass_of_air: {value: 0.01806, unit: kg, from: "P₁V₁/(R T₁) with R = 287 J/kg·K"}
  work_on_the_air: {value: 2.752, unit: kJ, cross_check: "m c_v ΔT = 2.754 kJ — the two routes agree"}
  what_happens_with_the_printed_R: >
```
