# Past Papers — index

> **Read me first.** This folder holds the actual assessment papers, transcribed verbatim and
> machine-readable. Use it to quiz him, to set timed mocks, or to work a question with him. Each paper file
> is self-contained and says what it maps to in the main KB — and, on this subject especially, what it maps
> to *nothing*.
>
> **Two rules that matter:**
> 1. `[q]` text is the paper's exact wording — quote it as printed. Where the paper is *wrong*, it stays
>    wrong in the `[q]` block and carries a `⚠ VERIFY` marker; the correction lives in
>    `../_verification-log.md` (§ Exam papers). Teach the correct form, and tell him the paper is wrong.
> 2. Where a question sits on a documented **gap**, say so out loud when teaching it, and label anything you
>    supply as `[added]`. Do not let missing theory drift into this KB looking like his lecturer's notes.
>
> **No paper in this folder has figures.** All three are text-only.

## ⚠ The gap map was right, and it was examined

`../00-index.md` § Gap map lists what the five MEC 3105 lecture documents never teach. **These three papers
examine almost every item on it.** That is the headline finding of this folder.

| Gap, as `00-index.md` records it | Where it was examined |
|---|---|
| **SFEE** — "❌ ABSENT FROM ALL FIVE DECKS … left as a documented gap rather than invented" | CAT 1 **Q3**; Exam **Q1F**, on the *compulsory* question |
| **Entropy as a property** — "⚠ PARTIAL. No general definition anywhere" | CAT 2 **Q1C** (3 marks); Exam **Q2A** (8 marks) |
| **Otto / Diesel / Brayton / Rankine efficiency** — "❌ ABSENT … a CAT risk" | CAT 2 **Q3A, Q3B**; Exam **Q5** (20 marks) |
| **Property / steam tables** — "❌ referenced but never reproduced" | four questions across two papers |
| **Vapour-compression refrigeration cycle** | Exam **Q4** (20 marks) — the phrase appears nowhere in the KB |
| **Availability / exergy** — *not currently listed in the gap map* | CAT 2 **Q1B** (3 marks) |

**Three things follow.**

1. **The `[added]` decisions already made were the right ones.** Named-cycle efficiencies, $dS = \delta
   Q_{rev}/T$ and Van der Waals were all supplied on the formula sheet against a predicted CAT risk. Every
   one of them turned up. Keep the `[added]` labels on rigorously.
2. **The SFEE should stop being an open gap.** `00-index.md` records it as deliberately left unfilled
   because GA2 needed it only for a discussion prompt. Two papers later it is compulsory-question material.
   Ask him whether a steady-flow handout exists; if not, supply it as `[added]` and say so every time.
3. **Availability/exergy needs adding to the gap map.** It is examined (CAT 2 Q1B) and the word appears in
   the entire knowledge base exactly once — inside TC s12's quoted definition of entropy, as
   "the *un*availability of a system's thermal energy". The nearest real coverage is EPC s84's
   "quality of energy" table, which is the same idea without the name.

## What the three papers weight differently

| Paper | Centre of gravity | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **CAT 1** (28 Aug) | First law, systems, definitions — **seven of eight parts are inside the KB** | ✅ **the best revision paper in the folder.** And Q1(B) is the V8 sign-convention trap set as an exam question |
| **CAT 2** (2 Oct) | Second law, entropy, COP, Rankine — half inside the KB, half not | a realistic CAT mock: it is the only paper whose marks add up |
| **End of Semester** (1 Nov) | Cycles and refrigeration — **the majority sits on gaps** | ✅ Q1 and Q3(A) as a mock; Q2, Q4 and Q5 need `[added]` material first |

**Two questions recur across papers, and one recurrence is exact.**

- **Heat pump vs refrigerator, 8 marks, twice.** The end-of-semester paper (Q1G) asks it bare — "What is the
  difference between a heat pump and a refrigerator?" The CAT 2 (Q2A) asks the same thing for the same 8
  marks but names the four headings: **primary function, direction of heat transfer, application, design**.
  Those four headings are the best guide available to what the 8 marks are for on *either* paper. Drill the
  CAT 2 version.
- **Entropy, defined and related to $Q$ and $T$** — CAT 2 Q1C (3 marks) and Exam Q2A (8 marks). Both land on
  the same partial gap.

**And a warning about where he revises entropy from.** ⚠ **TC slide 12 carries three separate verification
flags — V19, V20 and V21** ("entropy always increases with time"; enthalpy called "the total heat content";
"depends only on temperature, pressure and composition"). It is the most-examined topic in this folder and
the worst slide in the course. Teach it from `05-heat-engines-and-carnot` and `03b`, never from TC s12.

## ⚠ The 2025 papers hit the gap map harder than the 2024 papers did

The 2024 set already showed that MEC 3105's documented gaps were being examined. The 2025 set is worse.

| Paper | Marks on material the five lecture documents do not teach |

**The steady-flow energy equation can no longer stand as an open gap.** `00-index.md` records it as
deliberately left unfilled because GA2 needed it only for a discussion prompt. It is now worth **21 marks
across the 2025 set alone** — CAT 1 Q2, and the exam's Q1(b) and Q1(d), the latter two on the *compulsory*
question. Across 2024 and 2025 it has been examined on four separate papers. Supply it as `[added]`, keep
the label on it, and tell him every time that it is not in his notes.

**The Rankine cycle went from 5 marks to 20.** Named in TC s13, tabulated in HE s12, analysed nowhere.

**Two terms appear on the papers and nowhere in the knowledge base at all:** *"work ratio"* and *"specific
steam consumption"* — 4 marks, zero hits across every topic file, the formula sheet and the nomenclature.
Add them to the gap map.

## ⚠ Recurrence

**The 2025 CAT 1's Question 3 is the 2025 exam's Question 4(b), verbatim** — same three parts, same 5/5/2
allocation, eight weeks apart. Steam at 7 bar and dryness 0.95, expanding isothermally and reversibly to

**Steam at 7 bar turns up in five questions across the two papers.** If he drills one state point in this
unit, drill that one.

**Heat pump vs refrigerator** continues its run — the 2024 CAT 2's four-heading version (primary function,
direction of heat transfer, application, design) remains the best guide to what those 8 marks want.

## ⚠ No steam tables were photographed with any 2025 paper

The 3 Oct CAT's instructions say *"Where necessary, use the steam table attached."* No table is among the
photographs of any of the three 2025 papers, and the 2024 end-of-semester paper's promised appendix is
missing too (P7, P15).

**This blocks 18, 17 and 36 marks** on the three 2025 papers respectively, and the exam's Q5(b) additionally
needs R-134a tables. Combined with the knowledge base's own record that property tables are *"referenced but
never reproduced"*, this is the single most valuable thing he could supply. **Ask him for the tables.**

# House format — adding the next paper

The canonical statement lives in
[`../../../fluid-flow/knowledge-base/past-papers/00-past-papers-index.md`](../../../fluid-flow/knowledge-base/past-papers/00-past-papers-index.md)
§ House format, and `../../../docs/kb-format.md` governs the KB as a whole. The short version:

1. **Naming** — `<UNIT>-<TYPE>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`, using the code **as printed on the paper** even when it is
   wrong (the date disambiguates, and the `unit_code_note` explains). Figures, if any ever appear, go in
   `figures/<same-stem>-q<N>.svg`. Add a row to the Register and update Topic coverage.
2. **Frontmatter** — `kb, file_role: past-paper, paper_id, paper, unit_code, institution, date,
   duration_min, rubric, questions, total_marks, marks_reconcile, status, source, transcription, figures,
   errata_logged, kb_sections, tags`. Set `marks_reconcile: true` **only** after adding the printed
   part-marks and checking them against the stated total — on this subject that check has passed once in
   three.
3. **Transcription** — verbatim in `[q]` blocks, typos included; LaTeX for maths; never reconstruct
   unreadable text (record `legibility:` and ask him to re-shoot); a `→ KB section` mapping and the marks on
   every question; **and a `Gap?` column**, because on this subject that is the column that matters. Note
   any photo artefact — his own pen especially — that a future chat could mistake for exam content.
4. **Numerical claims** — any assertion that the paper's data is wrong, inconsistent or sound must be
   **computed, not asserted**. P5, P6 and P10 were each checked against real property data (CoolProp /
   IAPWS-IF97) before being written down; say in the entry what was checked and when.
5. **Errata** — `../_verification-log.md` **§ Exam papers**, IDs allocated continuously across the whole
   folder, referenced from the question by `⚠ VERIFY`. The full entry lives in the log only.
6. **Solutions** — `status: unsolved` by default. Worked solutions go in a separate
   `<paper_id>-solutions.md` so the questions stay usable as clean practice.
