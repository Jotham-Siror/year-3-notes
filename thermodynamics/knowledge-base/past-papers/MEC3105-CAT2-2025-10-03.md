### Q4a · 3 marks · → ❌ **the Rankine cycle is named in TC s13 and HE s12 and analysed in neither**

> [q] Describe the process of the Rankine cycle shown in Figure 2. *(3 marks)*

*Three marks for four processes: **1→2** reversible adiabatic pumping of saturated liquid to boiler
pressure; **2→3** constant-pressure heat addition in the boiler to dry saturated steam; **3→4** reversible
adiabatic (isentropic) expansion through the turbine; **4→1** constant-pressure heat rejection in the
condenser back to saturated liquid. ⚠ Take the process names from the figure but **the state of point 3
from the question** — see P19. And note the escalation: the 2 Oct 2024 CAT 2 asked "describe the Rankine
cycle and its significance in power generation" for **1** mark (erratum **P11** was about exactly that
under-pricing). It is now 3, inside a 20-mark question.*
